The maintainer reviews and merges pull requests, triages issues, cuts
releases and handles security reports (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

This is a spare-time project, so responses may take a few days. If you would
like to help maintain it, open an issue to introduce yourself after a few
contributions.
